Josie Lambert CSMT · CNMT

With over 20 years of experience, Josie brings skilled, attentive massage therapy to every session. She has been a Certified Sports Massage Therapist and Certified Neuromuscular Massage Therapist since 2004, and a Licensed Aesthetician since 2006.

Her background spans chiropractic clinics, medi-spas, health clubs, and five-star resorts, including Disney's Grand Floridian and Saratoga Springs. She combines that experience with a personal approach that starts with you.

Therapeutic-grade essential oils are incorporated throughout her work for a restorative touch.
